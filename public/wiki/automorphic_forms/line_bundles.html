Sections of a line bundle

We can view modular forms as sections of a line bundle on the modular curve.

Theorem 3.2.0.1.

There is a line bundle \(\mathcal L\) such that a modular form \(f \in \mathcal M_n\) is a section \(s \in H^1(Y, \mathcal L)\) where \(Y\) is the Satake compactification of \(\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb Z)\backslash \Gamma\).
